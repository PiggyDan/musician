Instrument samples used by MusicPath
=====================================

The MP3 files in this folder (piano, guitar, violin, cello, harmonica) are
rendered from the FluidR3_GM SoundFont, as published by the
midi-js-soundfonts project:
  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Attribution: FluidR3_GM SoundFont (Frank Wen and contributors), MP3 renders by
Benjamin Gleitzman (midi-js-soundfonts). A subset of notes (every third
semitone) is redistributed unmodified; MusicPath pitch-shifts between them.
The ukulele voice uses the nylon-string guitar recordings (General MIDI has no
ukulele program).
